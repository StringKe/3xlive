Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 18, 2026

1. What We Collect

When you sign in to 3X Live using a third-party provider (Google, X/Twitter, or Discord) via Clerk, we collect the following basic profile information provided by that provider:

  • Account ID from the provider
  • Display name
  • Email address
  • Profile image URL

If you choose not to sign in, we generate an anonymous player ID stored in your browser cookie. No personal information is collected in this case.

2. Third-Party Authentication

We use Clerk as our authentication provider. When you sign in, Clerk handles the OAuth flow with your chosen provider. Clerk may collect additional technical data as described in their privacy policy. We only receive the basic profile information listed above from Clerk.

Each provider has its own privacy policy governing the data they share:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • X (Twitter) Privacy Policy
  • Discord Privacy Policy

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Authentication: To create and identify your player account across sessions.
  • In-game display: To show your name and avatar on leaderboards and your in-game character.
  • Gameplay sharing: If you choose to share your game results to X/Twitter, we post on your behalf only when you explicitly click the "Share" button. This is never automated.

4. What We Do Not Do

  • We do not read your emails, tweets, Discord messages, contacts, or social media feeds.
  • We do not post, like, follow, or send messages on your behalf without your explicit action.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with any third parties.
  • We do not use your data for advertising or profiling.

5. Data Storage

Your profile information is stored securely on Cloudflare infrastructure. Authentication tokens are managed by Clerk and are not stored on our servers. Game data (scores, stats) is associated with your player account.

6. Data Deletion

You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us at privacy@3xlive.app. We will process deletion requests within 30 days.

7. Cookies

We use cookies for authentication sessions (managed by Clerk) and to store your anonymous player ID if you play without signing in. We do not use tracking, analytics, or advertising cookies.

8. Children

3X Live is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

10. Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, contact us at privacy@3xlive.app.
